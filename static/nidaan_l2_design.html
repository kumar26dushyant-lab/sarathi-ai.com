A claim can settle at any point

Most do not reach Lokpal. If the company pays during Reimbursement or after the grievance officer, the claim jumps straight to Completed — the later buckets are skipped, not marched through. The map is the longest possible road, not the compulsory one.

Annexure dates drive the alarm

No open-ended holds, ever

The form will not create one. A pause with no end date is exactly how a case disappears for a year, and it is the single most common way a bucket system rots.

03

Who is on a case

ClaimShield shows Manager · CP · Case Handler · Medical Officer. Taking your answer to Q8:

On the caseWhat it meansHow it is set
Origin
(was “Manager”)		 How the case came in — a trail, not a person to chase:
Subscriber raised — name Raised for subscriber — staff Raised by branch — branch Raised by staff — staff Direct		 Recorded automatically at intake. Never edited — it is history.
Channel PartnerThe CP whose share is paid at the end. Chosen at intake; a super-admin can correct it.
Case HandlerThe staff member currently doing the work. Optional. The bucket owns the claim; a handler is named only when a case needs one pair of hands.
Medical Officer
/ Advocate		 Approves the draft. Medical Officer on medical claims, Advocate on non-medical. Set when the claim enters Pending Draft. The field renames itself by claim type.
Why origin is a trail and not an owner

A marketing person who brought a case in six months ago should not appear to be responsible for drafting it. Separating where it came from from who is working it is what lets the bucket own the claim without losing the attribution that pays the partner.

Intake duty starts it → 📋 Live Cases
Anyone on Intake duty sees it under “paid, winnable, not started” and presses Start Level-2.
Remark written automatically: “L2 processing started by ANNAPURNA KASERA”
🔔 Telegram → whoever is on duty for Live Cases
The gist is built
Handler fills patient, complainant, hospital, admission/discharge, diagnosis, rejection reason and date, the claim email, consultation % and CF amount. The insurance company is picked from the list — not typed.
Claim type health → the health document checklist is generated: 11 papers.
→ 📄 Pending Docs · Waiting on complainant
3 of 11 papers are already on file. The complainant is asked for the rest, one at a time, on WhatsApp.
🔔 Complainant gets the first document request
The hospital refuses the indoor case papers
Sub-state changes to Waiting on hospital. The standard refusal letter is sent and recorded. Because this will take weeks, the handler parks it to Hold with a return date of D+30 and the reason typed in.
Remark: “14/09 [ SUHANA JAIN ] Hospital ne indoor papers dene se mana kiya — refusal letter bheja”
⏸️ Leaves the active list. Nobody is chased about it while it is parked.
Hold expires → back to 📄 Pending Docs, by itself
No one has to remember. The claim reappears flagged the pause has ended, at the top of the Pending Docs list.
🔔 Telegram → Pending Docs duty
Last paper arrives → 📄 shows Ready
11 of 11. This is the one thing the system can prove, so the claim shows as ready and moves to Pending Draft on one click — or automatically, if you switch that on.
→ ✍️ Pending Draft · Drafting
Gist and letter written in English and Hindi, side by side, as in ClaimShield. Sub-state → With Medical Officer. Dr. Ashish is the Medical Officer on this claim.
🔔 Dr. Ashish told the draft is waiting
Nothing has happened for 5 days
The bucket clock turns amber. The claim rises to the top of the Drafting list with a plain reason: “sent for approval 5 days ago, no answer”.

File couriered to Aditya Birla. POD number recorded. The 30-day clock starts.
Reminders are scheduled for day 10, 20 and 30 — sent by a person, shown on their list on the day.

Sub-state → Reimbursement Query. Because the waiting party flips from them to us, the claim jumps to the top of the handler's list immediately — their deadline now governs, not ours.
🔺 Highest priority until the query is answered

05

To and fro — every backward path

Forward is routine and needs no reason. Backward always needs a reason, because an exception nobody explained is how the same mistake repeats. Every move, both directions, writes a remark with the name of who did it.

From → ToWhy it happensWhat the system does
Pending Docs → Live CasesThe gist is wrong — wrong claim type, wrong rejection date, wrong company. The document checklist is regenerated from the corrected claim type. Papers already collected are kept and re-matched.
Pending Draft → Pending DocsA paper is unreadable, expired, or the wrong document entirely. That checklist item is un-ticked and the complainant is asked again for that one item.
Reimbursement → Pending DraftThe company returned the file on a defect in our own submission. Sub-state resets to Drafting. The 30-day clock is abandoned, not paused — a resubmission starts a fresh one.
Escalation → ReimbursementThe company reopens the file and asks for it again. A new 30-day clock. The escalation record is kept, so the second escalation is faster.
Lokpal → EscalationThe complaint is returned before registration for a defect. BHP number cleared. The one-year window keeps counting — it never resets.
Pending Payment → CompletedThe award figure was recorded wrong, or is challenged. Amounts unlocked for correction. Any fee already raised is flagged for review.
CP Payment → Pending PaymentThe customer's payment is reversed or disputed after the fact. Partner payment held. If it was already paid, it is flagged as recoverable rather than silently written off.
Any → HoldA genuine wait of weeks — hospital, court date, complainant abroad. Reason and return date required. Leaves the active lists; returns to its original bucket on the date.
Finished → anywhereClosed in error. Super-admin only, reason required, remark written. Deliberately awkward.
The one thing that never moves backwards

The one-year Lokpal window, counted from the insurer's rejection date. It is not affected by holds, by going back a bucket, or by the company reopening the file. Wherever the claim is, the days remaining are shown, and at 90 left it outranks everything in the office.

07

If this happens, then what

If…then…and if that fails…
The complainant stops replying on WhatsApp Sub-state → Waiting on complainant. Reminders taper: daily 3 days, then every 3rd day. After 14 days the branch or CP who brought the case is asked to reach them. After 21, the case is parked with a date.
The hospital refuses papers Refusal letter sent and recorded; sub-state → Waiting on hospital. Park to Hold with a date. It returns on its own — it does not quietly rot in Pending Docs.
The Medical Officer does not approve Amber at 5 days, and the claim tops the Drafting list with the reason spelled out. Red at 10 → sub-super-admins. They reassign the approval or chase directly.
The company neither pays nor replies Reminders on day 10, 20, 30 appear on the duty person's list on those days. Day 30 → the claim shows ready for Escalation and will not go quiet.
The complainant has no email and the stage needs one Flagged in words before the claim can leave Live Cases. The claim cannot enter Escalation without it — that correspondence has nowhere to go.
The duty person goes on leave mid-bucket Bucket flagged on duty but on leave above an empty bucket, with the return date. Super-admin rosters cover. Nothing is orphaned — the bucket owns the claims, not the person.
Two people work the same bucket Either can take a claim by putting their name on it; the rest stays a shared queue. If both take the same one, the second sees the first's name already on it.
A staff member leaves the company Duties removed, access severed the same hour. Their claims stay exactly where they are. Their remarks stay readable, with their name.
The Lokpal window drops under 90 days Top of every list, above everything, days remaining on the row. Daily to everyone on the case and all admins until it is filed or the case is closed.
Our fee is only part paid Sub-state → Part Payment, balance shown, stays on the chase list. Disputed Payment if the customer contests it — that is a conversation, not a chase.
A claim is moved to the wrong bucket Move it back with a reason. Both moves stay in the remarks. No approval needed. Correcting it immediately beats leaving it in the wrong queue.
Everyone is off and a bucket has red claims Coverage box names it to the super-admins before the day starts. Red claims still appear on every admin's list — work is never invisible just because it is unowned.
08

What gets built

  1. Buckets become data, not code — name, icon, colour, order, clocks, sub-states, fields, exit rules, allowed next buckets. This is the largest piece and the one that must be done carefully, because live claims move through it.
  2. The bucket pages — ClaimShield's layout: dense table, inline move buttons, sub-state tabs, search, Excel export, rows-per-page, colour per bucket.
  3. The case report — one page, every field above, the remarks log, the bilingual draft.
  4. The visibility layer — bucket age coloured and sorted, menu badges, the morning list, the escalation ladder. Partly built already.
  5. Intake duty — a new rosterable duty that can move claims from “paid, winnable, not started” into Live Cases.
  6. Medical Officer / Advocate as a real role on the claim, renaming itself by claim type.
  7. The bucket designer — the super-admin screen that edits everything in item 1.
Order I would suggest

Items 1–4 together, because they are one coherent change and splitting them would leave the team with half a workspace. Then 5 and 6, which are small. Then 7, once the shape has been used for a few weeks and you know which fields you actually reach for.
