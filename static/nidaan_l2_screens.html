Why forward asks so little

A reason is not required going forward — the person who did the work is simply reporting it, and making them justify finishing is how a system gets worked around. Backwards is the opposite, as Screen 6 shows.

Backwards always costs a sentence

The reason is mandatory, and it is not bureaucracy: it goes into the remarks, it goes to Suhana, and it is what the complainant is told when we ask for the paper again. An exception nobody explained is how the same mistake repeats next month.

What the system does with it

Only that one checklist item is un-ticked — 11/11 becomes 10/11. The other ten stay ticked, so nobody re-collects papers we already hold. The claim lands back in Pending Docs at 0 days, sub-state Waiting on complainant, and Suhana's phone buzzes with the reason in Dr. Ashish's own words.
